Fare Stash is an offline transit pass journal for people who use multiple transit tickets and cards. Record ride packs, time passes, or stored-value cards, log journeys manually, see user-entered remaining balances and expiration estimates, and track trip records for your own records. Works on Windows, Android and iOS phones and tablets. No account or location permission required. Fare Stash cannot purchase fares, scan travel cards, verify ticket validity, or replace official transit account balances.

Fare Stash is a planned offline transit pass journal for people who use multiple transit tickets and cards. Record ride packs, time passes, or stored-value cards, log journeys manually, see user-entered remaining balances and expiration dates, and export trip records for your own records. Intended for Windows, Android and iOS phones and tablets. No account or location permission is planned. This is roadmap copy: application features are not shipped yet. Fare Stash cannot purchase fares, scan travel cards, verify ticket validity, or replace official transit account balances.

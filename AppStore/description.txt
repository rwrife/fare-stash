Fare Stash is an offline transit-pass journal for Windows, Android, and iOS. Android and iOS support phones and tablets. Add ride packs, time passes, or stored-value cards; record rides and corrections; review user-entered balance and expiration estimates; restore a versioned JSON backup; and export ride history as CSV. Your records stay on this device unless you choose to export them. Fare Stash does not require an account or location permission and cannot purchase fares, scan travel cards, verify ticket validity, or replace official transit account balances.
